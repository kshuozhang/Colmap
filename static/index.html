A BETTER CAPTURE
Good photos make the model.
  1. Move the camera, keep the head still.Use a neutral expression. Have someone walk around you, with the head filling most of each photo.
  2. Cover every side.Aim for 80–150 overlapping photos at eye level, above, and below. Include ears, crown, back, and under the chin.
  3. Keep things consistent.Use one lens, soft lighting, no portrait blur or beauty filters. Hair and facial movement can create gaps.
